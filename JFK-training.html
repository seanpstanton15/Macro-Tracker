Race-day strategy

Start embarrassingly slow

Hike the road climb and every AT climb. Weverton switchbacks carefully — quads and Achilles. Nobody wins JFK on the AT; plenty lose it there.

Towpath run/walk

Run/walk from the start: 25 min run / 5 min walk at ~10:00–10:30/mi run pace. Conversational the whole way.

Fuel every 30 min

60–80g carbs/hr from the gun, no exceptions. Alternate Maurten with real food (PB&J, Uncrustables, potatoes, broth late). Garmin 30-min alert.

Carry your water

Bottle or soft flask always. Aid stations are refills, not the plan. ~16–24 oz/hr in cool weather. Salt only with water.

Layers & light

Cold start (~31°F) — layers you can stash. ~10-hr finish lands near sunset (~4:51 PM): headlamp or light in a drop bag.

The MCM lesson

3:19 at MCM: half split ~1:36 (within a minute of the half PR), last gel at mile 16, ~80 min with no carbs, no water cups miles ~20–22, wall at 22. Pace discipline + fuel every 30 min to the very end + carry your own fluid. Apply all three at JFK.
